# Section Templates Reference Map

English counterpart for `references/section-templates.ko.md`. Use this as the English navigation layer, then read the Korean original for the detailed Korean section language and category playbooks.

## Default Full Funnel

Use hero, pain, promise, proof, usage, detail, options, FAQ, and policy blocks unless the product, channel, or user request justifies a shorter structure.

## Fast MVP Funnel

For sparse inputs or quick drafts, use hero, three core benefits, three usage scenes, detail/components, and FAQ plus shipping/exchange.

## Category Playbooks

Select `category_playbook`, `style_template`, `figma_blocks`, and `claim_guard` before writing final copy or image direction.

Core categories covered by the Korean original:

- Beauty, skincare, and cosmetics
- Food, beverage, and health food
- Fashion and apparel
- Fashion accessories, bags, and jewelry
- Living, home, and kitchen
- Electronics and appliances
- Baby, kids, and pet
- Digital, service, and B2B

## Guardrail

The product page must not rely on generic product-level copy alone. The selected category playbook should change the evidence, compliance plan, section order, image cuts, and claim tone.
